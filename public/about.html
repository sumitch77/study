About us

Study smarter, one step at a time.

Highkey brings books, notes, practice questions, tests and previous year papers into one clean place, so you spend less time searching and more time learning.

§ 01

What is Highkey?

Studying is hard enough without hunting for material in ten different places. Highkey keeps it together: read a book, go through the notes, practise questions, check previous year papers, take a test, and watch your progress grow.

§ 02

Our ambition

We want Highkey to become one of the most helpful and honest places for students to learn: simple to use, fair to everyone, and always getting better.

Simple

A clean, fast place to study, with nothing in the way of your learning.

Fair

Honest tests and real scores, so your effort means something.

Always improving

New material, fixes and features, added regularly.
