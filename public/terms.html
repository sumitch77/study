§ 01

Acceptance

By creating an account or using Highkey, you agree to these Terms of Use and to our Privacy Policy. If you do not agree, please do not use Highkey.

Highkey is provided “as is” and “as available”. We work to keep it running well, but we cannot promise it will always be uninterrupted or free of errors.

§ 02

Your account

You can sign up with an email and password, or with Google. When you do:

  • Give us accurate information, and only use an email address that belongs to you.
  • Keep your password private. Never share it, or any sign-in or password reset link we email you, with anyone. Those links expire quickly and are meant for you only.
  • You are responsible for everything that happens through your account.
  • Tell us straight away if you think someone else has got into your account.
§ 03

Fair use of study resources

Books, notes, practice questions, tests, previous year papers and everything else on Highkey are there for your own personal study. You may not:

  • Copy, scrape or bulk download material, whether by hand, with scripts, bots or browser extensions.
  • Sell, repost or hand out our material to others, including on other websites, apps, groups or channels.
  • Share your account, or any premium access, with other people.
  • Get around limits, paywalls, rate limits or access controls, or help anyone else do so.
  • Send automated or repeated requests that slow the service down for other students.

Studying, revising and making your own notes from what you read are exactly what Highkey is for.

§ 04

Honest tests

Tests only help you if your score is real. Practice questions are for learning, so use your notes and books freely there. When you take a test, you must do it yourself. You may not:

  • Get answers from another person, a group chat or a website while taking a test.
  • Use AI tools, solver apps or scripts to answer test questions.
  • Take a test for someone else, or let someone else take yours.
  • Share test questions, answers or answer keys with others, or post them online.
  • Tamper with timers, scores, requests or results, or use a bug to gain marks.
  • Retake the same test through extra accounts to see or memorise the questions.
§ 05

One person, one account

Every person may have one Highkey account. You may not:

  • Create more than one account for yourself.
  • Create a new account to avoid a warning, a ban or any limit on your old one.
  • Use extra accounts to collect free access, trials or benefits more than once.
  • Make fake accounts, or sign up with someone else’s email address.
  • Run accounts for other people.
Lost access to your old account? Email us instead of making a new one, and we will help you get back in.
§ 06

Premium & refunds

Premium gives your account extra access to Highkey’s study resources. What is included, and the price, are shown before you pay. Premium is for you only. It cannot be shared, transferred or resold.

Cancel within 7 days

Full refund, no questions asked

If you change your mind, cancel within 7 days of buying premium and we will refund you in full. You do not need to give a reason. Email us from the address on your account, and we will send the refund to the payment method you used. How long it takes to show up depends on your bank or payment provider. When you cancel, premium access ends.

After the 7 days, premium is not refundable unless the law says otherwise.

What premium does not give you

  • Premium does not give you any right to copy, bulk download, share, post, sell or distribute our material. Everything in “Fair use of study resources” still applies to premium users.
  • You do not own the material. You are paying for personal access to it.
  • Cancelling or getting a refund does not let you keep copies. Delete anything you saved.

Fraud and stealing material

  • Payment fraud is not allowed. That includes paying with a card, UPI or account that is not yours or that you have no permission to use, and fake or false payment claims or disputes.
  • Buying premium just to take the material and then asking for a refund counts as stealing, even if it happens inside the 7 days.
  • If we find fraud, or stealing or distributing premium material, we may cancel premium straight away and ban the account and any linked accounts. You will not get a refund, and we may take any other action the law allows.
§ 07

Warnings & bans

We would rather help you get back on track than remove you. This is how it works when we find misuse of resources, cheating in tests, or extra accounts:

Step 1

Warning

We email you to say what we found. We may also remove the affected results, scores, extra accounts or access.

Step 2

Ban

If misuse continues or happens again after a warning, your account (and any linked accounts) can be suspended or permanently banned.

  • Serious cases, such as stealing or distributing our material, payment fraud, attacking or hacking the service, or harassment, can lead straight to a ban without a warning.
  • If you are banned for stealing material or for fraud, you will not get a refund for premium.
  • If you are banned, you may not create new accounts. Any new account made to get round a ban can be banned too.
  • Access to the content, progress and results on a banned account may be removed.
  • If you think we got it wrong, email us. We will take another look and reply.
