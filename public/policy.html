§ 01

Information we collect

We collect what we need to run Highkey, keep accounts safe, and keep tests fair. That includes:

  • Account details — your email, your username, and your password, which we store only as a hash and never as plain text. If you sign in with Google, we receive basic profile details such as your name and email from Google. We never see your Google password.
  • Study activity — the subjects and material you open, your progress, and your practice and test attempts, including answers, scores and timing.
  • Device & technical data — your IP address, browser and device type, request logs, and security events such as failed sign-ins.
  • Messages — anything you email or send us, and a record of the service emails we send you (sign-in links, password resets, warnings).
  • Premium purchases — your plan, purchase date, amount, payment status, and any cancellation or refund request. Your card or UPI details are handled by our payment provider.
§ 02

How we use it

We use your information to:

  • Create your account, sign you in, and show your progress and results.
  • Give you premium access and process purchases, cancellations and refunds.
  • Keep Highkey secure, stop abuse, and fix errors.
  • Enforce our Terms of Use, including the rules on fair use, honest tests and one account per person.
  • Send you service emails, such as sign-in links, password resets, warnings and important updates.
  • Understand what is not working and make Highkey better.

We do not sell your personal information.

§ 03

Fair-use checks

To keep tests meaningful for everyone, we may look at account and technical signals to spot extra accounts, automated access, answer sharing, or tampering. These signals can include:

  • Email address and sign-up details.
  • IP address and browser or device information.
  • The timing and pattern of test answers.
  • The volume and pattern of requests to the service.

If we find misuse, we first send a warning and then may ban the account, as described in the Terms. We keep a short record of warnings and bans (the email, the reason and the date) so that we can apply them fairly and stop banned users from coming back with new accounts.

Think we made a mistake? Email us and we will take another look.
§ 04

Premium & payments

When you buy premium, we collect your plan, the purchase date and amount, the payment status, and any cancellation or refund request. Payments are handled by our payment provider, which collects and processes your payment details under its own terms. We do not store your full card number or security code.

We use this information to give you premium access, to process cancellations and refunds (including the 7-day full refund), to help you if something goes wrong, and to meet accounting and legal requirements.

To protect honest buyers and our material, we may check for payment fraud and for theft of premium material. Signals can include payment status and disputes, repeated buying and refunding, and bulk downloading, copying or sharing of premium material. If we find fraud or theft, we may cancel premium and ban the account without a refund, as set out in the Terms.

We keep purchase and refund records for as long as accounting or the law requires.

§ 05

Storage & security

We use technical measures to protect your information. For example:

  • Connections to Highkey are protected with HTTPS.
  • Passwords are stored as hashes, not as readable text.
  • Sign-in and password reset links are random, work once, expire within minutes, and are stored in hashed form.
  • We limit how often sign-ins and reset requests can be made, to slow down guessing and abuse.

No online service can be perfectly secure. Keep your password safe, and never share your sign-in or reset links.

§ 06

Sharing & providers

We only share information when it is needed to run Highkey:

  • Service providers that host Highkey, store its database, deliver our emails, handle Google sign-in, and process premium payments. They may only use your information to provide those services, under their own terms and privacy practices.
  • Legal and safety reasons — when the law requires it, or when needed to protect users, the service, or our rights.
§ 07

Cookies

Highkey uses cookies to keep you signed in and to keep your session secure. These cookies are essential, so blocking them will stop sign-in from working.

If we add analytics or other optional tools, we will say so on this page.

§ 08

Retention & your choices

We keep your account information and study history while your account is active. Some technical logs are kept for a shorter time. You can ask us to:

  • Send you a copy of the personal information linked to your account.
  • Correct information that is wrong.
  • Delete your account and the data linked to it.
  • Stop optional emails. Service and security emails will still be sent.

Even after deletion, we may keep a minimal record of warnings and bans (email, reason and date) to stop banned users from returning, purchase and refund records, and any other records we are required by law to keep. We may ask you to confirm who you are before acting on a request.
